The four relays are one MyFamily, so Tor does not put them on the same circuit. Utilization is observed bandwidth divided by the rate configured in Tor. MB/s follows that configuration: 1024² bytes. Mbit/s is the unit Tor Metrics uses. Written and read totals sum the Onionoo history, average bytes per second times the interval. That is the operating period Onionoo still publishes, not a counter inside Tor. Depending on a relay's age, the longest history is a 10-day, 2-day, or daily average.
